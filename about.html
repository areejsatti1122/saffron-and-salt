About Us

A small family business that grew with the support of our customers.

Our Story

Saffron & Salt started in 2019 as a small biryani stall on weekends. People kept coming back for the same plate of biryani, so in 2021 we opened a proper restaurant in Gulshan-e-Iqbal.

Most of our recipes come from the family kitchen. We keep the menu short so that every dish is cooked fresh and tastes the same every time you visit.

What We Believe In

  • Clean kitchen and fresh ingredients
  • Treating every customer like a guest at home
  • Fair prices for families and students
  • Giving extra food to a local langar instead of wasting it

Our Journey

  1. 2019: Weekend biryani stall
  2. 2021: First restaurant opened
  3. 2023: Family hall and catering started
  4. 2026: Online orders and home delivery
